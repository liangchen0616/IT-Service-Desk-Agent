VPN Connection Troubleshooting Guide

Category: Network
Document ID: IT-NET-001

Problem:
Employees cannot connect to the company VPN.

Possible Causes:
1. Incorrect username or password.
2. VPN server is unavailable.
3. Network connection is unstable.
4. VPN client configuration is incorrect.

Solutions:
1. Check your username and password.
2. Restart the VPN client.
3. Check whether the VPN server is running.
4. Contact the IT support team if the problem persists.

Additional Information:
If the VPN connection continues to fail, create an IT support ticket and provide the error message.
